# Artwork in the public source distribution

Project code, line icons, the user-selected handwritten PS logo (concept15), procedural SVG frames and six Studio Originals are original project assets under MIT. Original designs: Arctic Glass, Kintsugi, Nocturne, Petal Atelier, Afterburn and Cloud Paws. Raster icons are generated from the same logo.svg.

The public frame catalog does not ship Steam Points Shop APNG binaries. Those belonged to a separate local reference comparison, not to the MIT artwork. Importing a user's PNG/APNG does not transfer or license the user's artwork.

`media/winter-forest.png` and `media/winter-lake.png` are the photographic references supplied by the user. `media/winter-forest-wide.webp` is a built-in image_gen photographic reconstruction of that forest: native1672x941, WebP quality94 without resizing. The exact prompt is retained under artwork/. The personal preview applies an adjustable dimming overlay, not a baked-in dark edit.

The optional `?showcase=psichopodobny` demo contains the user-supplied Arima portrait from Tokyo Ghoul. It is separate third-party imagery, not MIT-licensed original project artwork and not used as the neutral start or applied to unrelated accounts. Historic source images in older folders retain their respective rights. No GitHub, Valve, Steam or anime-rightsholder affiliation is claimed.

Manrope, Unbounded, JetBrains Mono and Cormorant Garamond are included unchanged with their SIL Open Font Licenses in media/fonts. Their original sources are https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl . Fonts are loaded locally and not installed into Windows.

Weather.js draws original leaves, droplets, sparks and snowflake sprites directly in canvas. No downloaded particle textures are used.
